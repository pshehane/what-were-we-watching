What this uses

Suggestions are built from your own library and nothing else. Turn off anything you do not want counted.
WHAT IT READS
Shows you finished
14 of them. The strongest signal there is.
Shows you shelved
6 of them. Used to rule things out, never to suggest.
Your wishlist
9 items. Two are watchable tonight.
Who you watch things with
So a suggestion for three of you is not picked from what you watch alone.

Off. Stalled shows will still be listed, just not ranked by this.

Anything about anyone outside this phone.
Where you are, beyond the country you pick yourself.

3 hidden. Tap to bring any of them back.
